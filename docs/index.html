Benighter PDF Document Scanner Privacy Policy

Published by Benighter. Effective 18 November 2026.

Documents and camera access

The app uses the camera when you scan a page or code. Scanned and imported pages, recognised text, saved signatures, scan history and settings are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. The scanner, text recognition and PDF tools work without an account. Android’s file picker and share sheet let you choose the destination for exports, backups and shared documents.

Google ML Kit

Google ML Kit performs text and barcode recognition on your device. The SDK can send device and app information, per-installation identifiers, feature usage, performance measurements and diagnostic information to Google over HTTPS for diagnostics and usage analytics. See Google’s ML Kit data disclosure and Google’s privacy policy.

Optional document AI in the direct-download edition

The direct-download edition offers optional Chat with PDF and Smart Auto-Naming. These features are off until you configure a service. Chat with PDF sends recognised document text and chat messages to the HTTPS service you connect. Smart Auto-Naming sends recognised text when you request a suggestion or enable automatic naming for new scans. A pairing token authenticates these requests. The supplied bridge sends requests to OpenAI to generate answers or filenames. The service operator and AI provider determine how they handle and retain requests. You can disconnect the service in the app’s settings.

Updates

Google Play handles updates for the Play Store edition. Other editions may request release metadata over HTTPS from Benighter’s release site and download an installer when you choose to update.

Retention and deletion

Local documents stay on your device until you remove them. You can permanently delete documents from Recently deleted, remove saved signatures and clear scan history in the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes the app’s local data. Exports and backups saved elsewhere can be deleted at their destination. For requests already sent to an AI service you connected, contact that service operator about retention or deletion.
